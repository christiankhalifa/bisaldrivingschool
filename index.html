How it works

Your route to a Class 5 licence

Theory comes first, then time on the road. Finish both to earn your course certificate.

STEP 1

Register

Send your details and choose a package. Bring your operator's licence number.

STEP 2

15-hour theory

Complete the online or in-class course within 3 months. Pass mark: 80%.

STEP 3

10 hours driving

In-vehicle lessons in our school car, finished within 6 months. Pass mark: 75%.

STEP 4

Road test

Book your test and tell your instructor ahead of time if you'd like to use the school car.

Terms & conditions

Good to know before you start

The key points from our student contract.

Certificate requirements
  • Minimum 10 hours driving and 15 hours in-class/online training.
  • Complete the in-class or online course first, then in-vehicle training.
  • Pass marks: 80% in class, 75% in driving.
  • Below 75% in driving: at least 2 more hours, as your instructor recommends.
Cancelling a lesson
  • Give at least 24 hours' notice to cancel a driving lesson.
  • Late cancellations are charged $50.
Time limits
  • Driving lessons: finish within 6 months of registration, or within 2 years with a $100 penalty.
  • 15-hour theory course: finish within 3 months, or up to 1 year with a penalty ($45 in-class, $75 online).
Switching course format
  • In-class to online after part 1: $75.
  • Online to in-class: $50.
Refund policy
  • Lessons taken are charged at $70/hr; the remaining amount is returned.
  • $75 is deducted if the in-class/online training has started or been completed.
  • $50 administration charge if you cancel before starting.
  • No refunds after 6 months from registration.
Pick-up, drop-off & passengers
  • Pick-up and drop-off are at your registered address. Discuss any change with your instructor before booking.
  • Training is in school vehicles only.
  • Passengers are limited to those allowed under Section 12(2), such as another student, an instructor or instructor-in-training, a school manager, or an interpreter if needed.
Road test vehicle
  • The school car for your road test isn't included in these packages.
  • Tell your instructor in advance where and when your test is booked. Availability depends on the instructor's schedule and area.
Records
  • Following Alberta Transportation standards, student records are kept for 2 years.
